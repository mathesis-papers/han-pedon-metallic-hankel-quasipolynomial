# Extending Han–Pedon (2026): proof of Conjecture C for metallic Hankel determinants (arXiv:2610.36132v1, p. 6)

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Extending Han–Pedon's lower-shift results (2026), we prove their Conjecture C (arXiv:2610.36132v1, p. 6): for every n ≥ 1 and ℓ ≥ n+3, every residue subsequence of the shifted q-metallic Hankel determinants is a rational polynomial, from its first term, with period 2n(n+1) for even n and 4n(n+1) for odd n. Each polynomial is integer-valued on all integers. A complementary-minor formula reduces the determinant to a matrix of fixed size with polynomial dependence on the number of continued-fraction periods. We give a degree bound, rational generating functions, constant-coefficient recurrences and periodicity from the first term modulo every positive integer at every shift. Exact complementary-minor identities and the degree bound prove the affine laws extending Ovsienko–Pedon's golden shift-4 pattern (2023; published 2025).
